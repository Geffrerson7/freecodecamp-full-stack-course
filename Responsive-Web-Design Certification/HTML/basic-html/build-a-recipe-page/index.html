Fluffy Pancakes

Light, golden pancakes made from pantry staples. They come together in about 20 minutes and are perfect for a slow weekend breakfast with butter, syrup, and fresh fruit.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the pan

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, beat the milk, eggs, and melted butter until combined.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir gently; a few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form, about 2 minutes.
  6. Flip and cook until golden, then serve warm with syrup and fruit.
