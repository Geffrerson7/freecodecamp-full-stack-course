Rise Beyond

Rise Beyond follows Ethan Carter, a street musician from a small coastal town who gets one unexpected chance to play on the biggest stage of his life. Between old promises, a band that never took off and a city that never slows down, he has to decide whether success is worth the person he used to be. A warm, guitar-driven drama about ambition, friendship and the songs we write to keep going.
