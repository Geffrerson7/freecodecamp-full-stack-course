Rio de Janeiro Carnival

Experience Brazil's biggest party!

The Rio de Janeiro Carnival is one of the most famous celebrations in the world. Every year, thousands of people gather to enjoy samba music, colorful costumes, parades, and the joy of Brazilian culture.

Parades, music, and fun

Enjoy the impressive samba school parades at the Sambadrome, dance to the rhythm of Brazilian music, and join the street parties known as "blocos". Get ready for an unforgettable experience full of rhythm, color, and tradition!
